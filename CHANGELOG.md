# Changelog

## 1.0 — October 2026

First release.

- Reference-list entries and parenthetical and narrative in-text citations in APA Style, 7th edition, for 14 kinds of source: journal articles, books, chapters in edited books, web pages, newspaper and magazine articles, reports and government documents, dissertations and theses, conference presentations, online videos, social media posts, data sets, dictionary and encyclopedia entries, and AI-generated content.
- Pages, paragraphs, section names, and timestamps in in-text citations, "as cited in" for secondary sources, and several works cited together in alphabetical order.
- Checks for common mistakes: titles not in sentence case, first-name-first authors, library database links, missing DOIs and URLs, quotations without a page, and more. A button puts a title into sentence case.
- A reference list that sorts itself and adds 2020a and 2020b where one author has two works in a year. Copy it, export it to Word or Markdown, or save it to a list file and open it again later.
- Runs entirely in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Light and dark themes.
